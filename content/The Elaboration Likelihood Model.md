Proposes there are two routes for reasoning, a central route and a peripheral one.
## Central route
- Happens when reader cognitively process the information
- Reader makes a critical assessment based off known facts and ideas
## Peripheral route
- The emotional route
- Opinions are formed based off cues like charisma and wit
