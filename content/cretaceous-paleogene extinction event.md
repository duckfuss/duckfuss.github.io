# Summary
- a mass extinction of 3/4 of all plant and animal species on earth ≈66mya
- apart from some ectothermic species like sea turtles, no 4 limbed vertebrae animals (tetrapods) weighing more than 25kg survived
- theorised to be result of asteroid impact
- preceded by the [[cretaceous|cretaceous]] era, and succeeded by the [[paleogene|paleogene]] era
# Causes
## Impact theory
- [![Meteoroid entering the atmosphere with fireball](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cb/Impact_event.jpg/164px-Impact_event.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Impact_event.jpg)
- resulted in an [[impact winter|impact winter]]
- made oceans acidic
- rendered large marine reptiles extinct
- 10-15km wide asteroid
- impacted into the Chicxulub crater in the Gulf of Mexico
