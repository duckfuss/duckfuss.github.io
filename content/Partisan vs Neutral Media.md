# For vs Against
#### Arguments For:
1. get specialised insight from people who look into and form informed opinions on your behalf
	1. normal people don't have the time or the synthesising ability to do this
	2. gives better viewpoint than otherwise
2. see both sides of the coin (if u read both)
3. an opinion is better than no opinion
#### Arguments Against:
1. inherently biased media is never going to present the full picture - only what they *want* you to see
2. there is always an agenda - no matter how subtle
	1. jason arday
	2. **profit**
3. spoon-feeding opinion is too easy - no critical thought is often required
	1. they make you feel how they want
	2. they are really good at writing so too persuasive (news should never be persuasive)
4. what is the point of news - to find facts about the world or form opinions?
	1. do we need to have an opinion about everything?
5. most of the journalists are professional opinion-havers, not highly trained specialists. These people are demonstrably partisan
6. even viewing multiple news sources - they may have to be neutral legally in *what they report* but they have freedom to **choose** what they report on
	1. see jason arday
