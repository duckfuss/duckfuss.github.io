# Uses of radiation
- [[NuclearMedicine|NuclearMedicine]]
- smoke alarms
	1. [[Alpha|⍺]] source that ionise air in the gap -> current across the gap
	2. smoke comes along, absorbs the [[Alpha|⍺]], preventing ionisation of the air in the gap
	3. this causes the current across the gap to drop -> sounding the alarm
- thickness monitoring in metal foil production
	- sends out [[Beta|β]] radiation, the amount that passes through foil depends on the thickness
