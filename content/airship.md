# Vacuum airship   ^feaad6
- a _hypothetical_ airship that contains a vacuum instead of a lighter than air gas
	- should work in principle
- problem lies in that the enormous imbalance of forces would cause the airbag to collapse unless it were incredibly strong
	- No strong material has been found that is sufficiently light enough 
- first proposed by italian  Jesuit  Francesco Lana de Terzi (known as the father of aeronautics) in 1670
  ![[u=https - - - www.oldbookillustrations.com - site - assets - high-res - 1837 - airship-1670-rawscan.jpg|208x247]]
- as the density of air is 1.28 g/l, 1 litre of displaced air has the buoyant force to lift 1.28g
