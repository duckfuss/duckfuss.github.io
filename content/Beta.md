# Beta radiation
## Properties:
- symbol β
- single electron ^c7c588
- -1 charge
- very fast
- 1m range in air
- stopped by thin sheet of metal
## What it is:
- fast moving electron emitted from the nucleus when a neutron turns into a proton
## Example
$$N^{14}_{7} \rightarrow X^{14-0=14}_{8} + ^{0}_{-1}β$$
