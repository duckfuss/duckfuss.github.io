# Summary
- a _model_ for [[quantum computing|quantum computing]] similar to classical circuits where a [[quantum algorithm|quantum algorithm]] is laid out as a sequence of [[quantum logic gate|quantum logic gate]]s, [[measurement|measurement]]s and initialisations of [[qubit|qubit]]s to known values etc.
## Notation
- time is on the X axis
- [[qubit|qubit]]s are represented as horizontal lines
- doubled lines represent classical binary bits
- items connected by these lines represent actions on the [[qubit|qubit]]s 
_N.B. lines represent sequence of events, not physical cables_
