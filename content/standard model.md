# summary
- theory of [[quantum mechanics|quantum physics]]
- describes the interactions of 3 of the 4 known [[fundamental forces|fundamental forces]]:
	- [[weak nuclear force|weak nuclear force]] 
	- [[strong nuclear force|strong nuclear force]] 
	- [[electromagnetic|electromagnetic]]
- describes the [[elementary particles|elementary particles]] and their properties like [[spin|spin]]
