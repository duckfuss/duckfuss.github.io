# Summary
- Founded in the first days of the city of Rome (around 753BC)
- Endured:
	- the overthrow of the [[Roman Empire|Roman]] Monarchy in 509BC
	- fall of the [[republic#Roman Republic|republic > Roman Republic]] in 1st Century BC
	- division of the [[Roman Empire|Roman Empire]] in AD 395
	- fall of western [[Roman Empire|Roman Empire]] in AD 476
- Politically weak for most of it's time
- apex of power by the mid republic
