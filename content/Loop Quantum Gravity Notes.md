- treats spaces and time as discrete quantities
- space isn't continuous but broken up into __quantas__ ($10^{-35}$m)
	- quantas are connected by links to form the 4D spacetime we experience

###### How it deals with Big Bang:
- Big bounce instead of big bang
	- Universe formed by the collapse of a previous universe

###### How it deals with black holes:
