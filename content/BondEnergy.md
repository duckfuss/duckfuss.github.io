# Bond Energy

## Making and breaking bonds
- __Making bonds releases energy__
- __Breaking bonds requires energy__
- when energy required to break bonds is ↑ than energy released when making bonds, the reaction is [[EndothermicReactions|EndothermicReactions]]
- when energy released when making bonds is ↑ than energy required to break bonds, the reaction is [[ExothermicReactions|ExothermicReactions]]

#### Bond energy definition
- energy required to break a bond between two atoms
## To calculate overall energy change:
1. work out energy required to break bonds
2. work out energy released when making new bonds
3. energy change = 2-1
