# Summary
- the testing or manipulation of a physical system to yield a numerical result
- generally, in [[quantum mechanics|quantum mechanics]] the predictions are probabilities
# Counterintuitive quantum measurement stuff
- [[wave-particle duality|wave-particle duality]] - a quantum particle acts like a wave in an experiment to measure wave-like properties, but acts like a particle in an experiment to measure particle like properties
- [[uncertainty principle|uncertainty principle]] means that you cannot measure exactly certain pairs of physical properties, i.e. you cannot know **both** the speed and position simultaneously.
- [[wave function collapse|wave function collapse]] occurs when a measurement reduces a [[wave function|wave function]], initially in [[superposition|superposition]] of multiple states into one.
