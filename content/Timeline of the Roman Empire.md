# Growth
- from 6th century BC to 27 BC Rome was a [[republic|republic]]
	- network of towns left to rule for themselves
	- varying degrees of independence from the [[Roman Senate|Roman Senate]]
	- Ruled by annually elected magistrates
- crises in the late 2nd century BC culminated in the assassination in 44BC of [[Julius Caesar|Julius Caesar]] ^63df6f
	- Expansion during the crisis![[Pasted image 20230217091409.png|Pasted image 20230217091409.png]]
- Mark Antony was defeated by Octavian at the battle of Actium in 31BC, leading, in 27BC to the beginning of the Principate (first epoch of [[Roman Empire|Roman]] Imperial history)
	- Octavius given title Augustus ("the venerated")
# Pax Romana
![[Pasted image 20230217092831.png|Pasted image 20230217092831.png]]
- 200 yrs that began with Augustus is regarded as _pax romana_
	- "[[Roman Empire|Roman]] peace"
- period of social stability and economic prosperity
### Dynasties
- Julio-Claudian Dynasty (5 emperors)
- Flavian Dynasty (3 emperors)
- Nerva-Antonine dynasty (produced the "five good emperors")
# Fall
- began with accession of emperor Commodus in 180AD
- after the [[Roman anarchy of the 3rd Century|Roman anarchy of the 3rd Century]]
- ![[Pasted image 20230217082917.png|Pasted image 20230217082917.png]]
- the empire split into the [[byzantine empire|byzantine empire]] and the [[western roman empire|western roman empire]]
