# Nuclear Fission
- net energy gain is only possible for elements heavier than iron
- splitting of an atomic nucleus
- less energy released compared with [[nuclear fusion|nuclear fusion]]
- lots of energy is released
	- [[Gamma|Gamma]] radiation
- spontaneous fission is rare
	- usually for fission to occur unstable nucleus must first absorb a neutron
- generates more energy than a chemical reaction as the [[Nuclear Binding Energy|Nuclear Binding Energy]] is greater than [[BondEnergy|BondEnergy]]
