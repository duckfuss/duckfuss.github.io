Use as a broadcasting station  
  id:: 63222c6c-8ce5-4eea-8823-a0f6837241c5
	- before the construction of the twin towers (late 1960's - early 1970's), most radio FM/TV stations broadcast from the Empire State Building
		- due to the taller towers reflecting radio waves broadcast from the empire state
	- soon, all broadcasters were broadcasting from the top of North Tower
	- after 9/11, nearly all broadcast stations were relocated back to the empire state building
	- after the One World Trade Center building, some have relocated there
- "race to the sky"  
	- original plan was for 50 stories, revised to 60, then 80
	- during that time there was intense competition for the title of the world's tallest building, known as the _race to the sky_
		- symbolic of the optimism of the 1920's
	- 40 wall street and chrysler building both vied for top spot
		- 40 wall street was revised in Apr 1929 to make it tallest
		- in response, the Chrysler building added a 56m steel tip in Oct 1929 to make it tallest
	- Chrysler building was revised to be taller than empire state
	- the empire state was then update, but standing only 1.2 m higher than the chrysler building, they were worried that chrysler would add another spire extention
	- so the empire state added a [[airship|airship]] mast to act as a station, giving it an 86th floor, and making it much taller than the Chrysler building
