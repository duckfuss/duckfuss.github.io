# Summary
- scientific theory relating space and time ([[spacetime|spacetime]])
## based off 2 main postulates that:
1. laws of physics are the same in all [[rest frame|inertial frames of reference]] 
2. speed of light in a vacuum is the same for all observers regardless of motion of light source or observer
