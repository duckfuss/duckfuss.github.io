- ## What is a Heptarchy?
	- the seven kingdoms of anglo-saxon England
	- known as the "[[Early Middle ages|Early Middle ages]]" or the "Dark ages"
- ## History of Heptarchy
	- from 5th century (end of [[Roman Empire|roman]] rule) until 8th century
	- 8th CE heptarchy was consolidated into 4 kingdoms  
		- Mercia
		- Northumbria
		- Wessex
		- East Anglia
