# Core Concepts
#### Strings
- As opposed to using point-based particles as in [[quantum mechanics|quantum physics]], string theory uses vibrating one dimensional strings
- these strings have a size of the planck length ($10^{-35}$ m)
- The resonant frequencies they vibrate at correspond to the fundamental particle they represent
	- most importantly, one of these fundamental particles is the __graviton__ (unifying quantum field theory with general relativity)
	- how it vibrates gives it it's mass/charge/etc. properties
- can be open or closed loops
- also posits that the 4 [[fundamental forces|fundamental forces]] were once a single [[fundamental forces|fundamental force]]

- "curled up" dimensions
