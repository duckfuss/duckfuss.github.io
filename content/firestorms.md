# Summary
- a fire which attains such intensity it can sustain its own wind system
- can be natural as result of wildfires or [[cretaceous-paleogene extinction event|cretaceous-paleogene extinction event]]
- can be man made, eg. the aerial firebombing of [[London|London]] and Dresden, as well as the [[nuclear bombs|atomic bomb]]ing of Hiroshima
