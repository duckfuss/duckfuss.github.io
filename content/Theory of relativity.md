#topic/physics  
# Summary
- composed of 2 main interrelated theories by Einstein
- superseded Newton's laws
- introduced ideas of:
	- 4d [[spacetime|spacetime]]
	- [[relativity of simultaneity|relativity of simultaneity]]
	- [[length contraction and time dilation|length contraction and time dilation]]
## [[special relativity|special relativity]] 
- applies to all phenomenon in the absence of gravity
- theory about the "structure of [[spacetime|spacetime]]"
## [[general relativity|general relativity]]
- explains the law of gravitation and its relation to the forces of nature
- applies to large-scale realm
