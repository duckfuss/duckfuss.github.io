# Summary
- under normal classical transformations properties like the time of an event or distances between events are constant, i.e. [[invariant|invariant]], no matter which [[Inertial frame of reference|IRF]] you look at it
- However these are not invariant in [[special relativity|special relativity]]! - so we want to find what the equivalent is!
