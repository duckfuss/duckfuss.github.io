# Gates
## Pauli gates (X,YU,Z)
- constitute rotations along the x,y,z axis of the [[bloch sphere|bloch sphere]] in $\pi$ radians
### Pauli X
- equivalent of a NOT gate
- maps $\ket{0}$ onto $\ket{1}$ and vice versa
