# Summary
- a subatomic particle that is not composed of other particles
- represented in the [[standard model|standard model]] as [[point particles|point particles]]

- [[fermions|fermions]]
	- [[quark|quark]]s
	- [[leptons|leptons]]
- [[bosons|bosons]]
	- [[gauge bosons|gauge bosons]]
	- [[scalar bosons|scalar bosons]]
