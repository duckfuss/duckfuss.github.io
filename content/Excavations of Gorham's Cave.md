# Excavations of Gorham's Cave  
- Discovery of four layers of stratigraphy
	- Level I
		- 8th to 3rd century BC use by phoenicians
	- Level II
		- brief [[Neolithic|Neolithic]] use
	- Level III
		- up to 240 [[Palaeolithic|Palaeolithic]] artefacts of [[magdalenian|magdalenian]] and [[soletrean|soletrean]] origin
	- Level IV
		- produced 103 items including:
			- spear points
			- knives
			- scraping devices
