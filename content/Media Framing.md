# Media Framing
>[!cite] To frame is to select some aspects of a perceived reality and make them more salient in a communicating text, in such a way as to promote a particular problem definition
> [[J-Communication-1993-Entman.pdf#page=2&selection=120,0,133,4|J-Communication-1993-Entman, page 2]]

>[!cite] "Cohen [1963] stated it eloquently: the press 'may not be successful much of the time in telling people what to think, but it is stunningly successful in telling its readers what to think about'."
