# Mole
- [[RelativeAtomic&FormulaMass|Mr]] or [[RelativeAtomic&FormulaMass|Ar]] in grams
- i.e. [[RelativeAtomic&FormulaMass|Ar]] of Carbon in grams is 12 g of carbon, which is a mole of carbon.
- A mole of any substance always has same number of atoms (Avogadro constant($6.02103\times10^{23}$))
-  $$ \mbox{number of moles} = \dfrac{mass(g)}{M_r}$$
