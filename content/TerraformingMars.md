## 3 step process
1) create the magnetosphere
	- stops deadly particle blasts from the sun (solar winds ([[RadiationOnMars|RadiationOnMars]]) which rip away Mars's atmosphere
	- could put satellite with very strong magnetic field between mars and the sun
2) Build the atmosphere
	- add CO2 to the atmosphere to the depleted [[AtmosphereOfMars|AtmosphereOfMars]]
		- could get CO2 from gas trapped in polar ice caps on Mars
			- drop nukes over ice caps???
			- not enough CO2
		- Build factories that release artificial greenhouse gasses
	- traps infrared light, warming up atmosphere (greenhouse effect)
3) release bacteria
	- absorb some of the nutrients on [[SoilOnMars|SoilOnMars]] and release oxygen into the atmosphere
	- wait between few 100 to few 1000 yrs
