# Summary:
- a large asteroid impact would eject an enormous amount of dust, ash etc.
- this would block the sun's radiation
	- preventing photosynthesis
# firestorms
- if the impact is large enough their might be an ignition of multiple [[firestorms|firestorms]], possibly with a global reach, further releasing ash, soot water vapour etc. into the atmosphere
