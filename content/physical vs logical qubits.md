# Summary
- a physical [[qubit|qubit]] is just a [[physical implementations of qubits|device that behaves as a 2 state qubit system]]
- a logical [[qubit|qubit]] is a [[qubit|qubit]] which performs a [[quantum algorithm|quantum algorithm]] and has a long enough  [[quantum coherence|quantum coherence]] time to be usable.
	- can be made up of physical [[qubit|qubit]]s
- Most qubit implementations need many physical [[qubit|qubit]]s for [[quantum error correction|quantum error correction]] to produce a logical [[qubit|qubit]]
	- see [[Google's plans to fix quantum computer accuracy issues|Google's plans to fix quantum computer accuracy issues]]
