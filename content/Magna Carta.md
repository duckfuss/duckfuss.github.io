## Summary
- [[Middle ages|medieval]] Latin for _"Great Charter of Freedoms"_
- founding of the British "constitution"
- ## what is the magna carta?
	- was drafted to make peace between the unpopular king John of England and a rebel group of barons
		- drafted by the Archbishop of Canterbury
	- it promised:
		- protection of church rights
		- protection for the barons from illegal imprisonment
		- access to swift justice
		- limitations on feudal payments to the crown
	- signed in runnymede, Windsor  
	- signed on 15th of June 1215 [[Late middle ages|Late middle ages]]
- ## history of the magna carta:
	- was introduced as a peace treaty
	- the magna carta was not followed by either side so was annulled by Pope Innocent III, leading to the [[first baron's war|first baron's war]]  
	- After John's death, Henry III reissued the document in 1216,
		- stripped of the more radical stuff
		- part of an *unsuccessful* bid to build political support for their cause
	- at the end of the [[first baron's war|first baron's war]], in 1217, it formed part of the peace treaty agreed at lambeth
	- re-issued in 1225 in exchange for a grant of new taxes
	- Edward I reissued it in 1297, confirming it as part of England's statute law
