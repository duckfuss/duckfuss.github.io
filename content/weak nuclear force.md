# Summary
- breaks [[parity conservation|parity conservation]]
- one of 4 [[fundamental forces|fundamental forces]]
- mediated by [[W and Z bosons|W and Z bosons]]
## Range
- less than the diameter of a proton!
# How does it work?
- [[W and Z bosons|W and Z bosons]] are exchanged by [[fermions|fermions]]
# What does it do?
- allows [[quark|quark]]s to swap their "flavour"
	- [[force carrier|mediated]] by the [[W and Z bosons|W and Z bosons]]
