- # 3 main impacts on whether a person's brain is creative/good at reading etc.
	- ## Nature
		- genes inherited from parents dictate characteristics
		- roughly 50%
	- ## Nurture
		- environmental factors (eg. upbringing) dictate characteristics
		- 17-0%
	- ## Random chance
		- random connections made in womb dictate characteristics
- # Exceptions:
	- Intelligence
		- upbringing is around 25% during childhood
		- why is this?
- # How studies isolate the difference between nature and nurture:
	- ## Identical twins v.s. non identical twins
		- former share DNA, latter share only 1/2
		- same upbringing is assumed
	- ## Biological siblings v.s. pairs of siblings where one is adopted
		- same upbringing
		- different genetics
- # Percentages:
	- Usually, due to [[nature vs nurture|nature vs nurture]] idea (without luck):
		- as 50% of characteristics come from genes, it is assumed 50% come from nurture
			- NOT true
			- around 17-0% come from genes
	- The other 33-50% comes from "non-shared environment"
		- often luck
