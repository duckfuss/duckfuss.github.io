# Demonstration 1
- If someone has just picked up some trash can they absolve themselves of needing to donate to a homeless guy?
- and vice versa: if someone declined to pick up trash are they more inclined to then donate
- looking to see if people [[moral licensing|moral license]], i.e. keep a "tab" of their morals.
	- if you do a good deed are you "done for the day"?
	- does guilty put ppl at a moral deficit
### Results:
- Many exhibited [[moral licensing|moral licensing]]
- only 2 did good deeds in both oppportunities
- interestingly many helped in *different ways*
- **definitely fitted the theory**
