# Summary
- The idea is that you can take, e.g. a [[Quetzalcoatlus|Quetzalcoatlus]], and, by looking at animals that share a common ancestor with it, derive common traits it might've had
	- so like a quealcoatlus would have more bird parts than croc parts![[Pasted image 20251213020926.png|Pasted image 20251213020926.png]]
	- but also soft tissue shared by birds and crocs likely also appear in [[pterosaurs|pterosaurs]]
