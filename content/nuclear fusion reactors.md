# nuclear fusion reactors 
- need to be very hot for fusion to take place 
- plasma state -> must be held in by large magnetic field 
- uses nuclear fusion for power 
	- hard to achieve
		- VERY hot plasma
			- must be contained at heat
			- must be contained for a long period of time
#### ways to increase efficiency 
- Plasma hotter 
- Plasma denser 
- increase confinement time
#### recent progress [[14th Sep 2022|14th Sep 2022]]
- reactor fires up for 30 seconds at 100 million degrees celsius
- no new time or heat record, but record time and heat
- lower density plasma was "key"
- was a sacrifice that boosted the other properties


# Nuclear reactors
- Uranium 235 and plutonium 238 commonly used
#### Control rods
 - lowered into the reactor
 - often made of boron
 - slow down the chain reaction by absorbing neutrons
