# Summary:
- period of prehistory between 3.3 million years ago to 11,650 [[BP|BP]]
- between earliest none use of stone tools by hominins to the end of the Pleistocene
- covers 99% of the period of human _technological prehistory_
- characterised by the development of **stone tools**
- preceded the [[mesolithic|mesolithic]] age
- hominins grouped together into small societies (eg. bands)
## Life subsisted of:
- fishing
- hunting
- gathering plants
- scavenging for wild animals
## Genetics
- humankind gradually evolved from early members of the genus _homo_ (eg. *homo habilis*, who used stone tools) into **anatomically modern humans** as well as **behaviourally modern humans**
### Climate:
- a set of interglacial and glacial periods
- fluctuated between warm and cool temperatures
## Locations
- Australia by 50,000 [[BP|BP]]
- Japan by 30,000 [[BP|BP]]
- Siberia by 27,000 [[BP|BP]]
- Americas, by the end of the [[Palaeolithic|Palaeolithic]] age , 11,500 [[BP|BP]]
